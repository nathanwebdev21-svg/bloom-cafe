Our Story

Bloom Café started in 2021 with one simple idea: create a place where great coffee brings people together. After noticing that many cafés felt rushed and crowded, we wanted to build a space where people could slow down, enjoy handcrafted drinks, and feel at home.

Today, Bloom Café is known for it's specialty coffee, freshly baked pastries, and warm atmosphere. Whether you’re catching up with friends, studying for an exam, or working remotely, we want every visit to feel comfortable and inspiring.

Current Owner: Sarah Mitchell
